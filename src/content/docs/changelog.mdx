## v0.1 <Badge text="Latest" variant="success" />

First public release.

- Chrome side-panel chat with history, model picker, and
  reasoning-effort selector.
- DevTools "Browy" panel with slash commands (`/page`, `/net`,
  `/console`, `/perf`, `/eval`).
- Native messaging host wrapping the GitHub Copilot CLI. Bring your
  own Copilot subscription.
- 30+ browser tools by default: snapshot, click, type, navigate, tabs,
  `evaluate_js`, network/console logs, sandboxed disk,
  persistent memory, `await_user`.
- Optional host tools (`run_script`, `bash`, `read_file`, `write_file`,
  `grep`, `glob`, `web_fetch`), off by default, enable per-tool under
  Settings → Tools. `run_script` joined them in 0.1.10. Since 0.1.14 the
  `read_file`, `write_file` and `bash` switches turn on the Copilot CLI's
  own tools: `view`, `create` and `edit`, and its shell, which is
  PowerShell on Windows.
- Multi-browser support: Chrome, Edge, Brave (and other Chromium
  builds).
- Stop ends a turn right away, and the chat picks up where it left
  off. Each window's Browy works on that window's tabs, and a message
  sent from a second window waits its turn (0.1.11).
- Chats are drawn straight from their Copilot session. Long chats open
  on their newest turns, the chats list opens at once, and Browy never
  deletes a chat on its own (0.1.14).
- Tools hold up on real sites: shadow DOM and same-origin iframes get
  indexes, rich-text editors take typing once, `extract_text` reads
  single-page apps, and a failing tool tells the model why (0.1.14).
- Browy recovers when the computer sleeps, the backend crashes, Copilot
  quits or stops answering, or a tab goes away, and says what happened
  (0.1.14).
- One-line PowerShell installer for Windows.
